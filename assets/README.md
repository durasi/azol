# The azoL logo

The capital L is a scraper. Its foot is a blade that slides under a layer of
rust, lifts it and breaks it up. Clean teal does the work; rust orange is what
goes away.

| File | Use |
|---|---|
| `logo.svg` | name and mark, on light backgrounds |
| `logo-dark.svg` | the same, on dark backgrounds |
| `mark.svg` | the L alone, square |
| `favicon.svg` | the L alone, simplified for 16 px |
| `compare/` | header tiles for the comparison table in the README: `azol.svg` carries the mark, `rust.svg` and `javascript.svg` carry the plain name only, in neutral grey that reads on light and dark backgrounds. They are our own drawings; no other project's logo is used |
| `alternatives/` | two other ideas that were considered |

## Colours

| | Hex |
|---|---|
| Teal (the L) | `#0B9BB0` |
| Rust (what is removed) | `#CE422B` |
| Ink (the letters, light backgrounds) | `#1B1F24` |
| Paper (the letters, dark backgrounds) | `#E6EDF3` |

## Rules

- Keep clear space around the logo of at least the width of the L's stem.
- `logo.svg` should not be shown less than 20 px high. Below 32 px use
  `favicon.svg` for the mark: the small fragments of `mark.svg` disappear.
- Do not recolour, rotate, outline or add effects. Rust is never the colour
  of the L.

## Construction

Everything is drawn on a 64-unit grid with straight lines, circles and
45-degree angles. The letters a, z and o are 9 units thick and 36 high; the L
is 10 thick and 50 high (12 in `mark.svg`). The rust layer is half as thick
as the L and separated from the blade by a sixth of its thickness.
